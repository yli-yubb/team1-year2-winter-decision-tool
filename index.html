Sources, method and assignment steps

Sources: Year 1 participant rules, Sections 2–11; Team 1 winter market screenshot; team-confirmed winter choices; user-selected spring–autumn plans and sales assumptions; approved hypothetical summer loan. The separate Year 2 homework brief defines this tool’s scope.

Year 1 scenario annual net profit: Sh 23,257. Closing cash: Sh 114,757. Debt: Sh 30,000. Tax loss pool: Sh 2,926. Machine 1 has four seasons left, Machine 5 six. Spring assumed sales of 55,000 are a modeling assumption, not a trainer-confirmed market block allocation.

Accounting: every monetary line rounds to the nearest whole shekel. Gross profit deducts all milk purchased, maintenance and depreciation. Bonus is 5% of positive gross profit. Rent, transport, market spending, salary and interest reduce pre-tax profit. Carried losses offset positive profit before 10% tax. Capital purchases and principal repayments affect cash, not profit.

Transport allocates sales in A–F production order; rounded residual goes to the last producing premise. Loan principal is rounded per installment, with the last payment clearing the remainder. Interest is charged on the balance before repayment. New loans make their first payment immediately in the borrowing season.

AI-assisted workflow: the assignment and team data were clarified in conversation, the brief was refined to distinguish actuals from assumptions and repair funding gaps, and Codex built and tested the tool.
